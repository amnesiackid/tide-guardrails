# Frequently Asked Questions

## Am I talking to a human?

No. This is an automated customer service chatbot. It may make mistakes, so please verify important information in the Help Center or with human support.

## Can I return an item I have used once?

No. Items must be never used, in their original packaging, with tags attached. An item that has been used, even once, cannot be returned. If it was defective, see the damaged or defective items policy.

## Can I return an item without the original packaging?

No. The original packaging is part of the return condition. Items without their original packaging cannot be returned, unless they arrived damaged or defective.

## How long do I have to return something?

30 days from the purchase date.

## Is there a restocking fee?

No, the restocking fee is 0%.

## How long does a refund take?

Refunds are processed within 5 days after the returned item arrives at our warehouse, and go to the original payment method only.

## Can I get my refund as store credit or to a different card?

No. Refunds go to the original payment method only.

## Can I exchange an item for a different size?

We do not offer direct exchanges. Return the item and place a new order for the size you want.

## How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping arrives within 7 days and expedited shipping within 2 days. International delivery usually takes 10 to 20 days.

## Is shipping free?

Standard domestic shipping is free on orders of $50.00 or more. Otherwise standard shipping costs $5.99.

## Do you ship internationally?

Yes, international shipping is available to most countries, including China and Germany, for a flat $19.99. Customs duties are paid by the recipient.

## Can I use two promo codes?

No. Only one promo code can be used per order, and discounts cannot be stacked.

## What is the biggest discount I can get?

No single promo code exceeds 20%. Loyalty members get 10%, which cannot be combined with a promo code.

## Do you have student discounts?

No, we do not currently offer student, military, senior or bulk discounts.

## I forgot my password. Can you reset it in the chat?

No. Passwords can only be reset through an email link from the **Forgot password** page on the login screen.

## My account is locked. What happened?

Accounts are locked after 5 failed login attempts. Reset your password with the email link to unlock it.

## How do I change my email address?

Go to **Account settings**. Two-factor authentication is required to change your email address.

## Can the chatbot check my order status?

No. The chatbot cannot see your orders. Check **Orders** in your account or use the tracking link in your shipping email.

## Is the chocolate gift box in stock?

The chocolate gift box is currently out of stock.

## Do your t-shirts come in different sizes?

Yes. Our t-shirts come in sizes XS to XXL. Each t-shirt product page includes a size chart with chest and length measurements.

## How do I talk to a human?

Use the contact form in the Help Center. Human support is available Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 CET.
