# Refund Policy

## When will I get my refund?

Refunds are processed within 5 days after the returned item is received at our warehouse. The refund processing time starts when the item arrives at the warehouse, not when you ship it.

After we process the refund, your bank or payment provider may need a few additional business days before the money appears on your statement. This depends on your bank and is outside our control.

## Refund method

Refunds are issued to the original payment method only. We cannot refund to a different card, a different bank account, store credit, or cash.

If the original card has expired or been closed, the refund is still sent to it. Your bank will normally forward the money to your new card or account; please contact your bank if it does not arrive.

If the order was paid partly with a digital gift card, the gift card portion is refunded back to the same gift card.

## What is refunded

- The price paid for the returned items, after any promo code or loyalty discount that was applied.
- The original shipping cost is refunded only if the item arrived damaged, defective, or was not the item you ordered.

There is no restocking fee, so there are no deductions for processing the return.

## Partial refunds

If you return only some items from an order that used a promo code, the refund is the discounted price actually paid for those items.

## Refund status

You can see the status of your refund under **Orders** in your account. You will also receive an email when the refund has been processed.
