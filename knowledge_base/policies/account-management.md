# Managing Your Account

## Creating an account

You can create an account at checkout or from the **Sign up** page. An account is not required to order, but you need one to track orders, request returns, and join the loyalty program.

## Updating your details

Your name, saved shipping addresses and saved payment methods can be changed under **Account settings**. Changing the email address requires two-factor authentication (see the account security policy).

Changing a saved address does not change the address on orders that have already been placed.

## Guest orders

If you ordered as a guest, you can track the order with the link in your confirmation email. To request a return for a guest order, use the **Guest returns** form in the Help Center with your order number.

## Deleting your account

You can request account deletion under **Account settings > Privacy**. Deletion is permanent. Open orders and returns must be completed first, and we keep order records as required by law.
