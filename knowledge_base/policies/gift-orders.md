# Gift Orders

## Sending a gift

At checkout, select **This is a gift** to:

- include a gift receipt, which shows the items but not the prices, and
- add a gift message of up to 200 characters.

Gift wrapping is available for $5.00 per order. Digital gift cards are delivered by email and are not gift wrapped.

## Returning a gift

Gift recipients can return items within the normal return window (30 days of purchase) if the items meet the return condition (never used, original packaging, tags attached). Use the **Guest returns** form in the Help Center with the order number on the gift receipt.

Because refunds are issued to the original payment method only, the refund for a returned gift goes to the person who paid for the order, not to the recipient.
