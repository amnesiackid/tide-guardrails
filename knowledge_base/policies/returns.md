# Return Policy

We want you to be happy with your order. If you are not, most items can be returned within 30 days of purchase.

## Return window

You can return an item within 30 days of the purchase date (the date the order was placed). Returns requested after 30 days cannot be accepted.

## Return condition

To be eligible for a return, the item must be:

- never used,
- in its original packaging, and
- with all original tags attached.

Items that have been used, even once, do not meet the return condition and cannot be returned. The same applies if the original packaging has been lost or thrown away, or if the tags have been removed.

If an item arrived damaged or defective, the return condition does not apply in the same way. See the damaged or defective items policy instead.

## Items that cannot be returned

- Food items (chocolate, coffee, tea, honey and gift baskets), unless they arrive damaged or defective
- Digital gift cards
- In-ear products (earbuds) and socks, unless unopened and in their original packaging

## How to start a return

1. Log in to your account and open **Orders**.
2. Select the order and choose **Request a return**.
3. Select the items and the reason for the return.
4. Print the return label and ship the item back within 14 days of requesting the return.

Return requests cannot be made through the chatbot. The chatbot can explain the policy, but the request itself must be submitted from your account.

## Return shipping cost

Return shipping is paid by the customer, unless the item arrived damaged, defective, or was not the item you ordered. In those cases we provide a prepaid return label.

## Restocking fee

There is no restocking fee. Restocking fee: 0%.

## After we receive your return

Once the item is received at our warehouse and inspected, your refund is processed. See the refund policy for timing and method.
