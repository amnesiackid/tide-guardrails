# Discounts and Promo Codes

## Promo codes

- Only one promo code can be used per order. Discount stacking is not allowed.
- No single promo code gives more than a 20% discount. The maximum discount is 20%.
- Promo codes must be entered at checkout and cannot be added after the order is placed.
- Promo codes cannot be exchanged for cash and cannot be used to buy digital gift cards.

The chatbot cannot create, give out, or approve promo codes, and it cannot grant discounts or free products.

## Loyalty discount and promo codes

Loyalty program members get a 10% loyalty discount. Because discounts cannot be stacked, the loyalty discount and a promo code cannot be combined on the same order. At checkout the larger of the two discounts is applied automatically.

## Gift cards are not discounts

A digital gift card is a payment method, not a discount. You can pay with a gift card and still use one promo code (or your loyalty discount) on the same order.

## Discounts we do not offer

We do not currently offer student, military, senior, or bulk discounts, and we do not price-match other retailers. If a product's price drops after you order, we do not refund the difference.
