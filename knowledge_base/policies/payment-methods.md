# Payment Methods

## Accepted payment methods

- Visa, Mastercard and American Express credit and debit cards
- PayPal
- Digital gift cards from our store

All prices are in US dollars (USD). Your card is charged when the order is placed.

## Paying safely

Enter payment details only on the checkout page. Never send card numbers, bank account numbers, or security codes in the chat. The chatbot cannot take payments and will never ask for payment details.

## Splitting a payment

You can combine one digital gift card with one card or PayPal payment on the same order. Splitting a payment across two cards is not supported.

## Refunds to your payment method

Refunds always go back to the original payment method. See the refund policy.
