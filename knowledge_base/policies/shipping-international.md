# International Shipping

International shipping is available. We ship to most countries worldwide, including China, Germany and the rest of the European Union, the United Kingdom, Canada, Japan and Australia.

## Cost and delivery time

- International shipping costs a flat $19.99 per order.
- Delivery usually takes 10 to 20 days after the order ships, depending on the destination and customs processing.
- The free shipping threshold ($50.00) applies to domestic standard shipping only, not to international orders.
- Expedited shipping is not available for international orders.

## Customs duties and taxes

Customs duties, import taxes and any fees charged by the destination country are paid by the recipient. They are not included in the order price or shipping cost, and we cannot predict the exact amount.

## Items that cannot be shipped internationally

Some items cannot be shipped abroad because of import restrictions, for example raw honey. The product page states when an item cannot be shipped internationally. These items are removed at checkout if your shipping address is outside the country.

## International returns

International orders follow the same return policy (30 days, never used, original packaging, tags attached). Return shipping from abroad is paid by the customer, and customs duties and taxes paid on import are not refunded by us.
