# Warranty

## What is covered

Electronics (earbuds, headphones, speakers, chargers, smartwatches, charging pads and desk lamps) come with a 1-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects, starting from the delivery date.

## What is not covered

- Accidental damage, such as drops, cracks or liquid damage beyond the product's water-resistance rating
- Normal wear, including battery capacity that decreases over time with use
- Lost items or lost parts
- Products that have been opened or repaired by anyone other than us

## How to make a warranty claim

Submit a warranty claim through the contact form in the Help Center with your order number, a description of the problem and, if possible, a photo or video. If the claim is approved, we repair or replace the product. If a replacement is not available, we refund the price paid to the original payment method.

## Warranty and returns

The warranty is separate from the 30-day return window. Within the first 7 days after delivery, a defective item can also be reported under the damaged or defective items policy.
