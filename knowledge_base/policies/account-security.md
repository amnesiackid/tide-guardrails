# Account Security

## Resetting your password

Passwords can be reset only through an email link. On the login page, choose **Forgot password** and enter the email address of your account. We will send you a link to set a new password.

Passwords are never reset, given out, or changed verbally, by phone, or through the chat. The chatbot and our human support agents will never ask for your password. If someone asks you for your password, do not share it.

## Account lockout

After 5 failed login attempts, your account is locked for your protection. To unlock it, reset your password using the email link from the **Forgot password** page.

## Changing your email address

Two-factor authentication is required to change the email address on your account. You will be asked to confirm the change with a code from your two-factor authentication method before the new email address is saved.

If you have lost access to your two-factor authentication method, contact human support through the Help Center.

## Suspicious activity

If you notice orders or changes you did not make, reset your password immediately and contact human support through the Help Center.
