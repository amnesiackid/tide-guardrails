# Privacy and Your Data in Chat

## The chatbot does not collect personal data

The chatbot is designed to answer general questions about our products and policies. It does not need, and does not store, your personal data. Please do not share personal information in the chat, such as:

- email addresses or phone numbers,
- passport, ID or driver's license numbers,
- social security numbers,
- bank account or card numbers, or
- passwords.

If a message contains information like this, it is automatically removed before the message is processed, and you are told that it was removed.

## Internal company information

The chatbot does not share internal company information, including employee details, internal contact details, or company bank information.

## Account-specific requests

The chatbot cannot see your account, orders, or payment details. For anything that requires your account, log in to your account or contact human support through the Help Center.
