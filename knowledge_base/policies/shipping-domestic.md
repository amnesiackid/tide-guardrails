# Domestic Shipping

## Shipping options

| Option | Delivery time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | within 7 days | $5.99, free on orders of $50.00 or more |
| Expedited | within 2 days | $14.99 |

Delivery times are counted from the day the order ships. Orders usually ship within 1 business day of being placed.

## Free shipping

Standard shipping is free on orders of $50.00 or more. The threshold is calculated on the order total after discounts and before shipping. Gift cards purchased in the same order do not count toward the threshold.

The free shipping threshold applies to standard shipping only. Expedited shipping is always charged.

## Delivery

Orders are delivered to the shipping address entered at checkout. We cannot change the shipping address once an order has shipped. If you need to change the address before shipping, see the order changes and cancellation policy.

We do not deliver to P.O. boxes with expedited shipping.
