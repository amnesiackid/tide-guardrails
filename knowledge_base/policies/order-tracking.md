# Order Tracking and Lost Packages

## Tracking your order

When your order ships, we send you an email with a tracking link. You can also see the order status and tracking link under **Orders** in your account.

Order statuses:

- **Placed**: we received your order.
- **Processing**: your order is being packed.
- **Shipped**: your order has left our warehouse; the tracking link is active.
- **Delivered**: the carrier has marked the package as delivered.

The chatbot cannot look up individual orders. Please check your account for the status of a specific order.

## Package marked as delivered but not received

Check with neighbors and around your delivery address, and wait one business day, since carriers sometimes mark packages as delivered early. If it still has not arrived, contact human support through the Help Center.

## Package not moving

If the tracking has not updated for 10 days (domestic) or 20 days (international), contact human support through the Help Center. We will open an investigation with the carrier and, if the package is confirmed lost, send a replacement or a refund to your original payment method.
