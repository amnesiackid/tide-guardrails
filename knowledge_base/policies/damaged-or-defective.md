# Damaged or Defective Items

If your item arrives damaged, defective, or is not the item you ordered, we will make it right.

## How to report a problem

Report the problem within 7 days of delivery using the contact form in the Help Center. Please include:

- your order number,
- a short description of the problem, and
- photos of the item and the packaging.

## What happens next

After we review your report, you can choose between:

- a replacement of the same item (if it is in stock), or
- a full refund to your original payment method, including the original shipping cost.

For most items we send a prepaid return label so you can send the damaged item back at no cost. For low-value or perishable items (for example food), we may ask you to dispose of the item instead of returning it.

## Food items

Food items cannot normally be returned, but if they arrive damaged, melted, spoiled, or with broken packaging, they are covered by this policy.

## Defects that appear later

Electronics that develop a manufacturing defect after the first 7 days are covered by the 1-year warranty instead. See the warranty policy.
