# Loyalty Program

## Benefits

Loyalty members receive a 10% loyalty discount on every order, applied automatically at checkout when you are logged in.

The loyalty discount cannot be combined with a promo code. If you enter a promo code, the larger of the two discounts is applied.

## How to join

Joining is free. Log in to your account, open **Account settings**, and turn on **Loyalty program**. You need an account to join; guest checkout orders are not eligible.

## Leaving the program

You can leave the program at any time from **Account settings**. Leaving does not affect past orders.
