# Contacting Human Support

The chatbot is an automated assistant. It is not a human, it may make mistakes, and you should verify important information with official sources such as this Help Center.

## When to contact a human

Contact human support if:

- your question is not answered by the Help Center or the chatbot,
- you need help with a specific order, return or refund,
- you want to report a damaged item or make a warranty claim, or
- you believe the chatbot blocked your message by mistake.

## How to contact us

Use the contact form in the Help Center. Choose a topic, add your order number if you have one, and describe your question. You will get a reply by email.

Human support is available Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 Central European Time (CET). We aim to reply within 1 business day.

For your security, never include passwords or full card numbers in the contact form.
